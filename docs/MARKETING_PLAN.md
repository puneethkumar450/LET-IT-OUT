# Launch and acquisition roadmap (not active in v0.1.0)

**Brand:** LET IT GO — “Talk freely. Feel heard. Find your calm.”

**Website:** A single-page preview is deployed through Sites. `let-it-go.in` has been purchased and is being connected; Play Store / future App Store badges and legal pages belong to the release plan. General contact: `contact@let-it-go.in`; product support: `support@let-it-go.in`.

**Channels to test after launch readiness:** Instagram Reels/Stories, Facebook, YouTube Shorts/Google App Campaigns, Snapchat, Moj, Josh; optional TikTok only in markets where available (not for an India-targeted campaign while banned). Social handles and paid accounts are not created by this starter.

**Starter content:** 15-second feature introduction, 20-second “difficult day” story, 30-second product walkthrough; warm colourful imagery and accurate demonstration. Do not use fake testimonials or imply the viewer is mentally ill.

**Draft test budget:** ₹9,000 Meta and ₹6,000 Google, only after closed-beta safety/privacy readiness. Treat as an experiment; no CPI guarantees. Check user acquisition **and** activation and 7-day retention.

**Privacy-safe events:** onboarding_completed, first_conversation_started (coarse count only). Do not send mood selected, message text, diagnosis, intensity rating, voice clips or AI reply contents to analytics/ad platforms.

**In-app ads:** NONE in V1. Avoid intrusive/personalized ads in emotional conversations even later; any monetization requires a separate safety and compliance review.
